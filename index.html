How the map is drawn

The rings come from a breadth-first search out of Grand Central: the fewest clicks it takes to reach each page. The thin connectors repeat that search from every page to every other page. Each link is then drawn as thick as the share of those shortest routes that pass through it, a measure called edge betweenness. Brandes' algorithm computes it with one search per page instead of comparing every pair.

Read Brandes, “A Faster Algorithm for Betweenness Centrality” (2001)
